# 🛒 Flipkart Sales & E-Commerce Data Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an **E-Commerce Sales Data Analysis** project built using Microsoft Excel. The objective is to clean, transform, analyze, and visualize a large-scale e-commerce dataset containing **80,000 product records**.

The project focuses on extracting meaningful business insights related to **sales, revenue, profitability, products, categories, brands, sellers, customers, ratings, discounts, and order performance**.

The analysis demonstrates practical **Data Analyst skills**, including data cleaning, data transformation, KPI development, Pivot Table analysis, dashboard creation, and business insight generation.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

* Analyze overall e-commerce sales performance
* Identify the categories generating the highest order volume
* Identify the most profitable brands
* Analyze revenue and estimated profit
* Understand product and seller performance
* Analyze customer ratings and reviews
* Evaluate discount patterns
* Analyze sales by city and category
* Identify trends across different years and months
* Create an interactive business dashboard
* Generate actionable insights from the data

## 🧹 Data Cleaning & Preparation

The raw dataset was processed to make it suitable for analysis.

### Data preparation activities included:

* Checked the raw dataset structure
* Cleaned and organized columns
* Created calculated fields
* Calculated discount amount
* Calculated revenue
* Calculated estimated profit
* Created rating segments
* Extracted month and year from listing dates
* Prepared data for Pivot Table analysis
* Validated calculated metrics
* Organized the dataset for dashboard reporting

### Key calculated fields

**Discount Amount**

```text
Discount Amount = Price × Discount %
```

**Final Price**

```text
Final Price = Price − Discount Amount
```

**Revenue**

```text
Revenue = Final Price × Units Sold
```

**Estimated Profit**

```text
Estimated Profit = Revenue × Estimated Profit Margin
```

## 🔑 Key Business Metrics

The project tracks important e-commerce KPIs including:

### 💰 Revenue

Total revenue generated from products sold.

```text
Revenue = Final Price × Units Sold
```

### 📦 Order Volume

Total number of units sold across the dataset.

### 💵 Estimated Profit

Estimated profit generated from sales.

### 🛍️ Average Order Value (AOV)

```text
AOV = Total Revenue ÷ Total Orders
```

### ⭐ Customer Rating

Average customer rating used to understand product satisfaction.

### 📈 Order Volume Growth

Used to understand changes in sales volume over time.

## 💡 Key Insights

Some of the major findings from the analysis include:

* **Toys** recorded the highest order volume with approximately **10,151 orders**.
* **Nike** was identified as a major contributor to estimated profitability, with approximately **₹97.12 billion** in estimated profit in the analyzed dataset.
* Revenue performance varies significantly across product categories and seller locations.
* Customer ratings and review volume provide additional indicators for evaluating product performance.
* Discount percentages have a direct relationship with final selling prices and therefore influence revenue calculations.
* Monthly and yearly analysis helps identify changes in business performance over time.

> **Note:** The insights are based on the dataset included in this project and should not be interpreted as actual Flipkart company performance data.
